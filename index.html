THE FEEDBACK LOOP

From sensing to delivery

  1. 01

    Sense

    The CGM estimates glucose in the fluid beneath the skin and sends readings wirelessly.

  2. 02

    Predict

    Control-IQ in the pump uses CGM data to predict where glucose is heading and adjust insulin delivery.

  3. 03

    Deliver

    The pump sends insulin from the cartridge through tubing and a tiny cannula under the skin.

The loop repeats as the CGM collects new readings. It’s hybrid because the student still manages tasks such as giving a meal bolus.
